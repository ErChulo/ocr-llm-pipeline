# Explicitly disclaimed source passages — conservative export guard

A literal model answer may be found inside source words that expressly say **“untrusted appendix,” “example only,” “sample not governing,” “not a governing provision,”** or **“do not use this example.”** Such a passage does not by itself establish an authoritative field value. The final export gate now rejects a reviewer-selected citation when its exact source line or short immediately preceding heading explicitly disclaims applicability. Original source bytes, SHA-256, text offsets, PDF page and OCR boxes stay unchanged. The user must select a separate authentic applicable passage or correct the proposed JSON.

A regular plan **Appendix A** is *not* automatically disclaimed, nor is the word “fictional” alone. This is deliberately a **narrow, conservative disqualification screen**, not automated legal applicability, an amendment priority engine, or general prompt-injection protection. It can still miss subtle examples and produce false abstentions on quoted non-governing language. Only final selected citations are blocked; AI proposal accuracy remains separately measured, and human source verification remains mandatory. Gate 3 and Gate 4 stay open.
