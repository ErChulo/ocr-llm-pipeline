## Unpassed production acceptance gates

1. Broader independently annotated real-format fictional scans beyond the measured nine-page cohort, including severe degradation, diverse fonts, multi-column tables, mixed native/raster content and unexpected page order. Prove **spatial cell association**, OCR omission/error rates and confidence calibration—not just token presence or same-line row proxies. The formerly missed ages 58 and 57 are recovered in the fixed nine-page fixture; broad unseen skew/rotation OCR accuracy and spatial cell provenance remain open defects.
2. Independent original-document review of geometric reading order, omitted annotation/form values, OCR duplication, and exact evidence-source consistency.
3. Broaden the now-reproduced 0.5B/1.5B eight-file and 0.5B/1.5B/3B two-file model tests: require adversarial abstention, field applicability, exact source-cell or text-region attribution, controlled performance/RAM and larger independent holdouts under representative device constraints. The observed injected-age failure is an unpassed safety/quality gate.
4. Run actual **offline file://** acceptance on an authorized managed Windows Chrome/Edge workstation; CI on a networked runner with browser requests blocked is not equivalent.
5. Independent privacy, source-security, supply-chain, accessibility and actuarial-interpretation reviews. Model-valid JSON and reviewer acknowledgements do not prove factual correctness.

No PBGC participant or case data is permitted in tests or the repository. ErChulo/bulk-ocr-studio must remain unchanged.
